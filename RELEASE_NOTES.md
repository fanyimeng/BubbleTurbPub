# Final-paper supporting-material refresh

- Updated the 118-row catalogue to the final global-ring densities, momentum,
  expansion times and supernova counts. The 118 CAND identifiers and native
  velocities are preserved. The separate 365-candidate CSV is unchanged.
- Updated the figure scripts and small input profiles to the final equations,
  including the 2.34e-24 g mean mass per H atom and the explicit Brinks corrections.
- Added both final submitted source-data workbooks and a figure-number driver
  for main Figures 1–4 and Supplementary Figures 1–14.
- Replaced personal FITS symlinks with documented data placement, provided a
  Git-only demo, and separated historical utilities from the final entry points.
- Refreshed expected PDFs from the validated scripts. Supplementary Figure 4's
  axis-ratio histogram now uses the final 118-row catalogue and agrees with
  `Supplementary_Data.xlsx`: bin counts are
  **46, 18, 16, 10, 7, 8, 6, 4, 1, 2**. The earlier submitted/static PDF used the
  historical catalogue (first bin 52). The curve in panel a is unchanged.

Main Figures 1–4 and the PV, consistency, ROHSA and factor-decomposition
composites were checked against the final artwork. Input-table calculations
were checked against the final catalogue and workbooks. The repository update
does not modify the manuscript or the publisher's submitted files.
